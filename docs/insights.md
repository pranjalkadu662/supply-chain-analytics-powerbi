# Key Insights

1. Demand is balanced across regions: East, North and South each sold about 0.46M units, West 0.45M.
2. Demand is seasonal: units sold peak in early 2024 and fall to a low around October 2024.
3. Actual demand (about 2M units) is above the forecast (1.83M), so the forecast underestimates demand by roughly 9%.
4. About 5K inventory records are below their reorder point, which is a stockout risk.
5. Average supplier lead time is about 8 days, so reorder points need at least that much buffer.

# Recommendations

- Raise the forecast or add a safety margin.
- Build stock before the early-year demand peak.
- Reorder earlier for items that often fall below the reorder point.
